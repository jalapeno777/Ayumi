## Recommendations

### Primary: **HeyGen Creator ($29/mo)**

**Rationale:**
1. **Unlimited videos** on Creator plan — best for high-volume TikTok production
2. **700+ AI avatars** — diverse, professional presenters
3. **Custom Digital Twin** — create consistent brand presenter
4. **TikTok-native** 9:16 format support
5. **Voice cloning** — consistent audio identity across videos
6. **4K export available** on Pro for quality content

**Concerns:**
- Free plan only allows 3 videos at 1 min each (not viable)
- 30-min video limit on Creator (fine for TikTok)

### Secondary: **InVideo AI ($15/mo Plus plan)**

**Rationale:**
1. **Lowest cost** viable option at $15/mo
2. **Native TikTok templates** — optimized for platform
3. **Fastest production workflow**
4. **Credits system** allows flexibility

**Concerns:**
- Credits-based (60 min/mo on Plus) vs unlimited
- Less professional appearance for financial content

### Alternative: **Pictory Professional ($35/mo)**

**Rationale:**
1. **Text-to-video directly from scripts** — minimal conversion needed
2. **600 video minutes/mo** — more content volume
3. **Getty/Storyblocks integration** — good for financial visuals
4. **Article-to-video** could repurpose blog content

**Concerns:**
- Avatar-focused content requires different workflow
- Less suitable for presenter-led forex education

## TikTok-Specific Considerations

### Vertical Video (9:16)
All evaluated tools support 9:16 TikTok format. Key consideration: ensure avatar/presenter is centered and visible in vertical crop.

### Video Length
- TikTok optimal: 21-34 seconds for forex education
- All tools can produce short clips
- HeyGen 30-min limit is more than sufficient

### Audio Quality
- Financial content requires clear, professional voiceover
- HeyGen and Synthesia have best voice quality (ElevenLabs integration)
- Pictory also offers ElevenLabs voices

### Chart/Screen Content
- Forex TikToks typically show chart screens with voiceover
- Synthesia has native screen recorder
- HeyGen supports screen recording integration
- Pictory can pull from URLs/articles
- InVideo has b-roll integration

## Technical Limitations & Considerations

### Avatar Realism
- AI avatars still have uncanny valley effect
- May need to test avatar selection for audience acceptance
- Custom Digital Twins (HeyGen) more natural but require recording

### Consistency
- Same avatar across videos builds brand recognition
- HeyGen's Custom Digital Twin best for consistent presenter
- Stock avatars vary between videos

### Script Adaptation
- TikTok scripts may need adjustment for AI video format
- AI video tools work best with conversational, presenter-style scripts
- Screen-heavy content (chart analysis) requires different approach
