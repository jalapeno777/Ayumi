## Question
What is the TAM/SAM for an AI voice receptionist SaaS targeting SMBs in HVAC, plumbing, and dental verticals? Which vertical has the highest willingness-to-pay?

## TAM / SAM Sizing

### Top-down
- **Voice AI market:** $3.14B (2025) → projected $47.5B by 2032 (getnextphone.com, Apr 2026)
- **AI SaaS market:** $22.21B (2025) → $30.33B (2026) → $367.6B by 2034 (Fortune Business Insights)
- **AI receptionist sub-segment:** Estimated 5-8% of voice AI market in 2026 → ~$150-240M

### Bottom-up (SAM — SMB HVAC + Plumbing + Dental, North America)

| Vertical | SMB count (NA, est.) | Missed-call cost/call | Annual loss/SMB | WTP/month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HVAC** | ~120,000 businesses | $300-$500 (Service Roundtable benchmarks) | ~$259K (5-tech company) | $99-$299 |
| **Plumbing** | ~150,000 businesses | $200-$400 (estimated from revenue/call) | ~$39K (solo) to ~$120K (small fleet) | $49-$199 |
| **Dental** | ~200,000 practices | $653 per missed new patient call | ~$50K-$100K+ | $99-$299 (HIPAA premium) |

**SAM estimate:** ~470,000 SMB businesses × $150/month average = **~$846M/year addressable revenue**

### Penetration assumption
Current AI receptionist penetration in SMB trades: <2% (early adoption phase). Realistic 3-year capture: 3-5% → **$25-42M ARR for a category leader**.

## Vertical Selection: Highest-Opportunity Segment

**Ranking by revenue-per-call × missed-call-volume:**
1. **HVAC** — Highest revenue per call ($300-$500 blended, $450-$1,200 top performers). Seasonal demand spikes (summer/winter) create acute missed-call pain. High urgency: AC breakdowns = same-day revenue.
2. **Dental** — Highest per-call value ($653/new patient) but lower call volume per practice. HIPAA compliance is a barrier to entry (moat if solved). Recurring appointment model favors AI scheduling integration.
3. **Plumbing** — Lower per-call value but very high urgency (emergency calls). Good entry vertical but lower ARPU.

**Recommendation:** Lead with HVAC. Highest pain-per-missed-call, clear seasonality story, fragmented competition.

## Willingness-to-Pay Benchmarks

- Current SMB AI receptionist pricing: **$49-$299/month** (guptadeepak.com, 2026 comparison)
- ROI threshold: At $99/month, HVAC business needs to catch **<1 call/month** to break even (at $300-$500/call value)
- Reported ROI: 75% reduction in missed calls, 300% first-year ROI (guptadeepak.com)
- Lead conversion improvement: 49% → 70% with AI receptionist (guptadeepak.com)
