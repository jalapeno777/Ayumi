# Opportunity Brief: Trading Journal SaaS for Prop Firms

**Date:** 2026-07-24
**Author:** Satsuki (Research Director)
**Consumer:** Yumeko (Opportunity Pipeline), Himari (Portfolio)
**Confidence:** MEDIUM — market growth data solid, user-count proxies weak (prop firms don't disclose), competitive pricing verified

---

## Question
What is the market size for a trading journal SaaS targeting prop firm traders? What are the growth signals for TradeZella/Edgewonk, and is there willingness-to-pay for AI-specific features?

## Market Context: Prop Firm Industry

### Growth trajectory
- **Search volume** for "prop firm" terms: **+5,525% increase** 2020-2026 (tradeify.co, Jan 2026)
- **Global payouts** by prop firms to traders: **>$325M in 2025** (tradeify.co)
- **Market structure:** Migrating from unregulated Forex/CFD models to regulated Futures prop firms
- **Accessibility:** Evaluation accounts cost $97-$251 one-time; instant-funded $350+ (cost-to-access ratio >1:450)

### User-count proxies (INDIRECT — prop firms don't disclose funded counts)
- FTMO historically claimed ~500 funded traders per cohort, ~30% survival rate → implies ~1,700 challenge participants per cohort
- FundedNext reportedly competing with/surpassing FTMO in 2026 (tradeinformer.com, Feb 2026)
- Major firms: FTMO, FundedNext, Topstep, Tradeify, MyFundedFutures, The Prop Firm
- **Estimated total funded traders globally:** 50,000-150,000 (high uncertainty)
- **Estimated total challenge participants:** 500,000-2,000,000 (based on ~10% pass rate)

### Multi-account behavior
- Prop firm traders average **2-4 accounts** across different firms (tradezella.com/blog/prop-firm-trading-journal, Apr 2026)
- Each account has different drawdown rules, daily loss limits, and profit targets → journaling complexity is real

## SAM Sizing (Bottom-Up)

| Segment | Est. users | ARPU/year | Market size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Funded prop traders | 50,000-150,000 | $360 ($30/mo × 12) | $18M-$54M |
| Challenge participants | 500,000-2,000,000 | $180 ($15/mo × 12, lower conversion) | $90M-$360M |
| **Total addressable** | | | **$108M-$414M** |

**Realistic 3-year capture for a focused entrant:** 2-5% of funded segment + 1-2% of challenge segment → **$5M-$15M ARR**

## AI-Feature Willingness-to-Pay

### Existing AI features in category
- TradeZella: "AI insights" (included in $29-$49/mo — not a premium upsell)
- Others: Limited AI features

### Potential AI features with WTP
1. **AI trade-pattern detection** — identify recurring mistakes (overtrading, revenge trading, FOMO entries). WTP: +$10-$20/mo on top of base.
2. **AI strategy optimization** — suggest parameter adjustments based on journal data. WTP: +$20-$50/mo for serious traders.
3. **FTMO rule-aware journaling** — auto-track daily loss limits, max DD, profit targets per prop firm. WTP: +$10/mo (convenience feature).
4. **AI coaching** — personalized feedback after each losing streak. WTP: +$20-$30/mo.

**AI premium tier potential:** $49-$99/month (vs. current $29-$49 base). Premium tier could capture ~20-30% of serious traders.

## Risk Assessment

**Risk 1: TradeZella dominance.** 500+ broker integrations is a significant moat. New entrants need deep broker coverage to compete.

**Risk 2: Free alternatives.** PlanningTrade offering 100% free journal. If sustainable, compresses market pricing.

**Risk 3: Niche size.** Funded trader population (50K-150K) is small. Challenge participant churn is high (~90% fail rate → many don't stick with journaling).

**Risk 4: Prop firm bundling.** Prop firms could bundle journaling into their platforms (some already have basic analytics).

## Recommendation
**Marginal pursue.** Market is real but small ($108-$414M total, $5-15M realistic for a new entrant). TradeZella is entrenched with broker integrations. The AI-feature wedge is thin unless paired with deep prop-firm-specific workflows (FTMO rule tracking, multi-account management, challenge prep analytics). Best angle: **vertical depth** — a journal built specifically for prop firm challenge preparation and funded-account management, not general trading.
