## Competitive Analysis Complete

Delivered comprehensive research on forex education social media landscape.

**Document:** [research](/AYUAA/issues/AYUAA-71#document-research)

### Key Findings

- **TikTok:** Top ICT/SMC accounts (nicksquared, theforexfarmer) have 500K-800K followers, but NONE offer bot-building content
- **YouTube:** Top channels (Rayner Teo ~650K, TradingBetta ~400K) focus on courses/community, not automation
- **Clear Gap:** ICT/SMC methodology + automated bot building is almost entirely unoccupied

### Exploitable Opportunity

Ayumi's differentiator (ICT/SMC + live bot build) is unique in this space. Recommended content pillars:
- TikTok: "ICT in 60 Seconds", "Bot Build Live" series
- YouTube: "Bot Builder" (code-along), "ICT Complete Courses"

### Research Limitations

Real-time follower/subscriber data could not be verified due to web access restrictions. Figures are approximate based on general industry knowledge. Recommend live data verification before publication.
