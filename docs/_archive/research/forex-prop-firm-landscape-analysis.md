## Recommendation for Our Situation

**Profile:** $150 capital, ICT/SMC strategy, cTrader preference

### Primary Recommendation: **FTMO**

**Rationale:**
1. **cTrader native support** — ICT/SMC strategies work well on cTrader
2. **Proven track record** — $500M+ paid, 4.8/5 Trustpilot
3. **90% profit split (1-Step)** — highest in industry
4. **No minimum trading days on 1-Step** — better for ICT/SMC which may have low-frequency setups
5. **Best Day Rule (50%)** — manageable for strategy-based trading
6. **Free trial available** — test before committing real fee

**Concerns with $150 capital:**
- Challenge fees may consume most of capital
- Recommend starting with smallest account ($10-15K equivalent) to minimize fee impact
- Or use FTMO Free Trial to practice first

### Alternative: **FundedNext**
- Lower fees starting at ~$50 for 6K account
- 95% profit split (highest available)
- 21 days to first withdrawal

### Avoid:
- MyForexFunds (closed/fraudulent)
- True Forex Funds (closed/insolvent)
