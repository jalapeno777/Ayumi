## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Issues & Solutions

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|------------|---------|-------------------|
| Low referral uptake | Medium | Medium | Offer $5 bonus for first referral, create referral contests |
| Telegram adoption issues | Low | High | Exclusive content, early access to signals, community highlights |
| Email deliverability problems | Low | High | Use reputable service, monitor deliverability, optimize content |

| Technical issues | Low | High | Thorough testing, backup plans, quick response team |
