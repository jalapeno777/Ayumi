FL-006: NYC Reversal Day Trade (Post-UK Exhaustion Reversal) What this setup isThe NYC Reversal Trade is a session-based reversal trade that occurs early in the New York session, after price has already completed its primary move during Asia and UK.This setup is designed to capture:exhaustion, not continuationmean reversion, not expansionIt is traded against the prior move. When this setup is even allowed This setup has a hard session rule.✅ Time windowThe reversal must form within the first three hours of the New York session.If no reversal structure appears in that window → stand down. Critical prerequisite (this defines the setup) This rule is non-negotiable:❌ UK must NOT have taken the Asia High or Asia LowIf UK takes Asia High or Low → you are looking for NY continuation, not reversalIf UK fails to take Asia High or Low → NY Reversal is allowedThis rule alone eliminates most false signals. Market conditions you want to see Before a NYC Reversal can occur, price should show clear exhaustion traits:Price is at Level 3 (extended move already completed)The High and Low of the day are already formedPrice has pulled away from the moving averagesMomentum is slowing, not acceleratingThis is a market that has already done its job. What typically happens on the open NY open often includes manipulation before reversal.This manipulation can look like:a single stop-hunt candlea short fakeout sequence (a few candles)a brief push that fails to continueYou are not trading the manipulation – you are waiting for failure. The reversal trigger The trigger is a clear reversal signal forming inside the NY window.Common triggers include:candlestick reversal patterns (e.g. railroad tracks, bearish or bullish engulfings)M or W formationsstrong rejection after a manipulation move (stop hunts)You are trading failure and rejection. Targeting logic Primary targets are mean-reversion based.Typical targets:50 EMAmiddle of the daily rangeKey levels like HOD, LOW etcIf those align with:a Market Maker candleprior imbalance recoveryor a higher EMA (e.g. 200)…that strengthens the trade.The goal is recovery, not a full trend reversal. Why EMA targets matter here When price reverses in NY, the Market Maker is often:recovering inefficient candlesrebalancing prior imbalancereturning price to “fair value”That’s why EMA and range-mid targets work so consistently for this setup. What this setup is NOT not a breakout tradenot a trend continuation playnot something you trade late into NYnot valid if UK already took Asia liquidityThis is a timed reversal, not an all-session idea. Common mislabels Trading NY reversal when UK already swept AsiaTrading continuation candles instead of exhaustionEntering too late (after the first 3 hours)Expecting a large trend change instead of mean reversionIgnoring manipulation before the reversal How this shows up in Discord When someone says:“This might be a NY reversal”The correct checklist is:Did UK fail to take Asia High/Low?Are we inside the first 3 hours of NY?Is price clearly extended and away from EMAs?Are we seeing rejection, not continuation?If not – it’s not this setup.
