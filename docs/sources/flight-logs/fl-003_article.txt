FL-003: Multi-Session M / W Formation (Within the Asia Range) What this setup isThe Multi-Session M or W is a reversal setup where the first part of the formation is created during Asia, and the second part completes across the Asia → UK transition, without breaking the Asia High or Low.M formation = short biasW formation = long biasThe defining feature is that price fails to repeat the Asia extreme, signalling exhaustion and reversal. When you’re allowed to look for it (Day Filter) This setup uses the same qualification rules as the other Asia → UK strategies.✅ Rule 1: Asia range must be under 2%Measure Asia High to Asia Low< 2% = acceptable> 2% = no trade day✅ Rule 2: Asia must be consolidationYou want:sideways price actionmultiple touches of highs and lowsno clean trend from start to endTrending Asia reduces the reliability of this setup. How the formation develops This setup unfolds over more than one session.For an M formation (short)First peak forms during Asia (near Asia High)Price pulls away into the end of Asia (profit-taking phase)During the UK Gap or early UK, price attempts to revisit the highThat attempt fails to break the Asia HighReversal behaviour appears For a W formation (long) Same structure, inverted:First low forms during AsiaPrice pulls away late AsiaUK attempts to revisit the lowFails to breakReversal behaviour appearsThe range must remain intact. What confirms the setup You’re looking for failure and rejection, not a breakout.Common confirmation signals include:wicks into the Asia High / Lowinability to close outside the rangeengulfing candles against the attempted moveclear rejection at the extremeIf price accepts outside the Asia range, this is not the setup. The trigger logic The trigger occurs after the failed attempt to revisit the Asia extreme.Entry is taken once rejection is clearThis often happens in the first hour of UKYou do not need to wait for deep continuationThe key is recognising that the second leg of the M/W has failed. Stop loss placement Your stop goes:above the first peak in an M formationbelow the first peak in a W formationThe first peak is always the structural reference point. Targeting logic Typical targets include:a key high or low from the last 1–2 sessionsa nearby EMAinternal Asia range liquidityThis setup often produces larger R multiples than the single-session version when it plays cleanly. Mandatory exit rule Regardless of outcome:✅ You must be out before the last hour of UK(around 8:00am New York time).This setup is not designed to run into US volatility. Common mislabels Confusing this with a liquidity grabTrading it when Asia already broke structureEntering before rejection is clearIgnoring that the Asia range must remain unbrokenTreating a failed attempt as continuation How this shows up in Discord When someone says:“This could be a multi-session M”The correct questions are:Did the first peak form in Asia?Did UK fail to repeat that extreme?Did the Asia range remain intact?Are we still early enough in UK?If not – it’s not this setup.
