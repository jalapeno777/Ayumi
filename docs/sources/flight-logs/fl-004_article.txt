FL-004: Multi-Session M / W Fakeout (Asia Range Break & Failure) What this setup isThe Multi-Session M / W Fakeout is a reversal setup where price breaks the Asia range, attracts breakout traders, and then fails to hold outside the range, completing an M or W formation before reversing back through the range.This is not a continuation breakout.It is a false expansion followed by rejection.Fake break above Asia High → short biasFake break below Asia Low → long bias When you’re allowed to look for it (Day Filter) ✅ Rule 1: Asia range must be under 2%Measure Asia High to Asia Low< 2% = acceptable> 2% = stand down✅ Rule 2: Asia must be mostly consolidationThis setup is more fragile than the others.You want:sideways or compressed Asiano strong directional trendno clean expansion before the fakeoutIf Asia already expanded aggressively, fakeouts lose meaning. How the fakeout forms This setup unfolds across multiple sessions.For an M fakeout (short setup)A first peak forms during AsiaPrice pulls away late Asia / early UKDuring early UK, price attempts the Asia HighThat attempt fails to hold, completing the MReversal follows For a W fakeout (long setup) The structure is inverted:First low in AsiaPull awayReturn to Asia lowFailure to holdW completes → reversalThe key feature is failure after expansion. What qualifies as a valid fakeout A valid fakeout usually shows:a brief acceptance outside the Asia rangeinability to continue in breakout directionwicks or rejection candles near the extremeno structural follow-throughIf price accepts and builds structure outside the range, this is not a fakeout. The trigger logic The trigger is the failure of the second attempt.You are not trading the initial breakYou are trading the failed continuationEntry occurs once rejection is clear and momentum shiftsThis often appears before 5:00am New York time. Stop loss placement Your stop goes:above the first peak in an M fakeoutbelow the first peak in a W fakeoutThe first peak remains the structural anchor – not the breakout wick. Targeting logic Common targets include:the opposite side of the Asia rangea nearby EMAa recent key level from the last 1–2 sessionsinternal range liquidityThis setup often delivers larger R multiples than standard Asia-range trades when it plays cleanly. Mandatory exit rule Regardless of outcome:✅ You must be out before the last hour of UK(around 8:00am New York time).This setup is not designed to transition into US. Why people mislabel this setup Treating the initial break as the tradeConfusing it with a Liquidity GrabIgnoring that a second failure is requiredTrading it when Asia already trendedHolding into US expecting continuation How this shows up in Discord When someone says:“This might be a fakeout M”The correct checklist is:Did Asia qualify?Was there a real break of the range?Did price fail to continue after the break?Did the second attempt fail?Are we still early enough in UK?If not – it’s not this setup.
