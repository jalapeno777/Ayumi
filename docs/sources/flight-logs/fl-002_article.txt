FL-002: The Liquidity Grab (Asia Range Stop-Hunt Reversal) What this setup isThe Liquidity Grab is a reversal setup that occurs when price briefly breaks the Asia range, grabs liquidity (stops), and then fails to continue, leading to a move in the opposite direction through UK.This setup relies on trap behaviour, not breakouts.Break below Asia Low → look for reversal upBreak above Asia High → look for reversal down When you’re allowed to even look for it (Day Filter) This setup uses the same Asia qualification rules as the other Asia → UK strategies.✅ Rule 1: Asia range must be under 2%Measure the Asia High to Asia Low< 2% = acceptable> 2% = no trade day✅ Rule 2: Asia must be consolidationYou want:sideways behaviourmultiple touches of high and lowno clean directional trendIf Asia trends, liquidity grabs are less reliable. Where and when it usually appears The Liquidity Grab most commonly forms:in the last hour of Asiaduring the UK Gapor within the first 30–60 minutes of UKYou typically have a narrow window to act.This is not a slow, patient setup. What qualifies as a liquidity grab A valid liquidity grab has all of the following:A candle breaks the Asia High or LowThat break does not holdThe candle shows:a large wicka small bodyThere is often a volume spike compared to surrounding candlesPrice closes back inside the Asia range or fails to continue in the breakout directionThis is the market running stops, not starting a trend. What it is NOT This setup is not:a clean breakouta continuation movea full-bodied close that holds outside the rangea structure break that leads to follow-throughIf price breaks and accepts outside the range, this is no longer a Liquidity Grab. The trigger logic Once the stop-hunt candle closes:You are looking for immediate reactionEntry happens within the next 15 x one-minute candlesIf you wait for the next full 15-minute candle to close and it’s already moving → you are lateThis setup rewards decisiveness, not perfection. Lower timeframe behaviour (optional but common) Many traders drop to the 1-minute timeframe to fine-tune entry:If the next 1-minute candle immediately moves away → market entry is often requiredIf price briefly retraces, you may get a slightly better entryYou generally have no more than ~14 minutes after the stop-hunt candle closesIf momentum goes without you – that’s fine.Missing this trade is better than chasing it. Stop loss placement Your stop goes:beyond the wick of the liquidity grab candleon the opposite side of the stop huntThis setup is invalid if price returns and runs that liquidity again. Targeting logic Common targets include:the opposite side of the Asia rangea recent high or low from the last 1–2 sessionsa nearby EMA (often the 200)This setup often delivers ~2R.Occasionally more, but that’s not the expectation. Mandatory exit rule Regardless of outcome:✅ You do not hold this trade into the last hour of UK.You want to be flat before 8:00am New York time.Holding into US defeats the purpose of the setup. Why people mislabel this setup Treating a real breakout as a liquidity grabIgnoring volume and candle structureEntering too late after momentum has already goneExpecting this setup to appear every dayForcing it when Asia wasn’t consolidating How this is used in Discord When someone says:“This looks like a liquidity grab”The correct checklist is:Did Asia qualify?Was there a clean stop hunt beyond the range?Did price fail to accept outside the range?Are we still early enough in UK to act?If not – it’s not this setup.
