FL-001: Single Session M / W Formation (Asia → UK Day Trade) What this setup isThis is a single-session M or W formation that forms entirely inside the Asia range, and gives you a day trade that usually plays out into UK so you can be done before US volatility.W formation = long biasM formation = short biasThe key is: both peaks form inside Asia.No breaking the Asia High/Low required for this one. When you’re allowed to even look for it (Day Filter) Before you label anything “Single Session M/W”, the day must qualify.✅ Rule 1: Asia range must be under 2%Measure the Asia range from the high to the low.< 2% = tradable day> 2% = day off✅ Rule 2: Asia must be consolidation, not a trendYou want:multiple touches to the highmultiple touches to the low“even highs / even lows” feelno clean directional push from start to endIf Asia is trending, this setup is lower quality. Asia measurement window (important) For this family of strategies you measure Asia like this:Start: 8:00pm New York time (includes the Gap Zone portion)End: 1:30–2:00am New York timeYou exclude the final hour of the full Asia session because it’s often profit-taking / bounce behavior and can distort the range.So you’re measuring the “clean” Asia range, not the whole thing. The formation rules (what it must look like) For a W (long setup)Inside the Asia range:Price prints a first low (first “peak” of the W)Price moves up toward the top of the Asia rangePrice pulls back and prints a higher lowThat higher low is your trigger point For an M (short setup)Same idea, inverted:First highDrop awayRetrace to a lower highThat lower high is your trigger point The trigger (this is the whole setup) You do not enter on the higher low candle itself.✅ Entry is on the next 15-minute candle after the candle that creates:the higher low (W)the lower high (M)Condition: that next candle must not break past the second peak.In a W: it must not break below the second lowIn an M: it must not break above the second highIf it breaks → it’s not the setup. Timing expectations This setup often forms like this:First peak forms in the first half of AsiaSecond peak forms in the second half of AsiaEntry can appear earlier than the last hour of Asia, but typically around the Asia → UK transition windowIf you don’t get an entry within your active window (roughly 2am–5am NY), treat it as a day off rather than forcing it. Stop loss placement Your stop goes:under the first peak of the Wabove the first peak of the MNot the second peak.The first peak is the “anchor.” Targeting options (choose one) This is a day trade setup – most of the time it’s not going to be a 4:1 or 5:1 RR hero trade.Default target:The top of the Asia range (for W longs)The bottom of the Asia range (for M shorts) Optional stretch targets if there’s still time left in UK:a nearby EMA (example: 200 EMA)a key swing level from only the last 1–2 sessionsdon’t go hunting levels from 3+ sessions back for this setup Mandatory exit rule Regardless of what happens:✅ Do not hold into the last hour of UK.You want to be out before 8:00am New York.Even if you didn’t hit target – you close. Common ways people mislabel this setup Asia was trending, not consolidatingRange was >2% and they traded anywayThe formation didn’t fully form inside AsiaThey entered on the higher low candle instead of the next candleThey held into US and got reversed when they weren’t watching How to use this in Discord If someone says “this looks like a Single Session W” the correct response is:Does Asia qualify? (<2% + consolidation)Is the W/M fully inside Asia?Are we entering on the candle AFTER the higher low / lower high candle?Are we out before the last hour of UK? If any answer is no → it’s not this setup.
