### Summary

3-phase, 60-day plan to reach 100 active members:

- **Phase 1 (Days 1-14):** Foundation — channel optimization, welcome upgrade, moderation guidelines, daily engagement hooks. Target: 25 members
- **Phase 2 (Days 15-35):** Growth — organic acquisition across TikTok (primary), Twitter/X, Reddit, Instagram Reels. Content escalation with weekly deep dives and educational drops. Target: 60 members
- **Phase 3 (Days 36-60):** Momentum — referral system, partner cross-promo, live screen-share sessions, member-generated content spotlight. Target: 100 members

### Key Decisions

- **$0 additional spend** — all organic, zero budget
- TikTok as primary funnel, aligned with [AYUAA-50](/AYUAA/issues/AYUAA-50) content calendar
- Source tracking via #introductions survey to optimize channel allocation
- KPIs tracked weekly: member count, DAU/MAU, message volume, retention
