## Email Configuration Notes

### Personalization Variables
- `{first_name}` - User's first name
- `{referral_code}` - User's unique 8-character referral code
- `{click_count}` - Total clicks on referral link
- `{conversion_count}` - Number of signups from referrals
- `{conversion_rate}` - Conversion percentage
- `{direct_earnings}` - Commission from direct referrals
- `{indirect_earnings}` - Commission from indirect referrals
- `{total_earnings}` - Total referral earnings
- `{pending_payouts}` - Pending commission balance
- `{leaderboard_rank}` - User's position in referrer leaderboard

### Subject Line Optimization
- Keep under 50 characters for mobile previews
- Use emojis sparingly but effectively
- Include clear value proposition
- Personalize with user's name when possible

### Timing Best Practices
- Email 1: Immediate (upon signup)
- Email 2: 24 hours later (user engagement high)
- Email 3: 48 hours later (reinforce community)
- Email 4: 96 hours later (referral conversion)
- Email 5: 168 hours later (weekly recap, establish pattern)

### Deliverability Tips
- Use reputable email service (SendGrid, Mailgun, AWS SES)
- Authenticate domain with SPF, DKIM, DMARC
- Monitor deliverability rates and engagement
- Clean email list regularly
- Provide easy unsubscribe option

### A/B Testing Opportunities
- Subject line variations (emoji vs. no emoji)
- Send time optimization (morning vs. evening)
- Content length (short vs. long form)
- CTA placement (top vs. bottom)
- Personalization level (first name vs. generic)
