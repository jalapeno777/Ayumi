### What the plan covers

- **Competitive analysis** — 5 leading crypto communities and lessons for Piggy Bank Academy
- **Enhanced onboarding** — 5-minute, 24-hour, and 7-day touchpoint flows
- **5 content pillars** — daily market analysis, build updates, educational posts, trade ideas, weekly challenges
- **Engagement tactics** — QOTD rotation, accountability groups, voice rooms, gamification roles
- **Week 1-4 KPIs** — targets from 25 to 200 members, daily active users, message volume
- **Free-to-premium path** — Phase 1 (build value, weeks 1-8), Phase 2 (soft launch premium at $29/mo, weeks 8-12), Phase 3 (scale with affiliates)
- **Revenue projections** — conservative $0 to $2,900/mo over 6 months
- **Cross-division coordination** — Kai (build updates, forex-crypto funnel), Media Manager (TikTok repurposing), Nori (OKR tracking)
- **Weekly execution checklist** — specific action items for each of the 4 weeks
- **Risk mitigation** — slow growth, low engagement, spam, burnout, premium launch failure
